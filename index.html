Hermes Home Alerts

Private SMS notification program operated by Robert Desselles, Folsom, Louisiana

Hermes Home Alerts sends automated text messages from a home automation server to members of the Desselles household only. Messages include door and garage alerts, security and power alerts, server status, and scheduled household reminders. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Use this form to request text alerts. Your request is reviewed by the program owner before your number is added.

After your number is added, you will receive this confirmation text:
"Hermes Home Alerts: You're subscribed to home alerts and reminders. About 1-10 msgs/week. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

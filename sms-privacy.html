Hermes Home Alerts – Privacy Policy

Hermes Home Alerts is a private home automation notification program operated by Robert Desselles.

What we collect: Only the mobile phone number of each household member who gives consent, along with the date of that consent.

How it is used: Phone numbers are used only to send the automated home alerts and reminders described in the SMS Terms.

Sharing: No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the SMS provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time. Your number will be removed from the notification list.

Retention: Numbers are kept only while the recipient remains opted in.

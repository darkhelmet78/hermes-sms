Hermes Home Alerts – SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Program

Hermes Home Alerts is a private, non-commercial SMS notification program operated by Robert Desselles, Folsom, Louisiana. Messages are sent from (985) 615-2937 by a home automation server.

2. Message types

Automated home alerts and reminders, such as door or garage alerts, security and power alerts, server status, and scheduled household reminders. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

3. How to opt in

Household members opt in by submitting the form at https://darkhelmet78.github.io/hermes-sms/ and checking the SMS consent box, or by texting START to (985) 615-2937. After opt-in you receive a confirmation text.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Expect about 1–10 messages per week.

5. Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

6. How to opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to opt back in.

7. Help

Reply HELP for help, or email rdesselles@gmail.com.

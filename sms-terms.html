Hermes Home Alerts – SMS Terms & Conditions

Program: Hermes Home Alerts is a private, non-commercial notification program operated by Robert Desselles. It sends automated text messages from a home automation server at phone number (985) 615-2937.

Who receives messages: Only the owner and members of his household who have given verbal consent in person.

Message types: Automated home alerts and reminders, such as door or garage alerts, security and power alerts, server status, and scheduled household reminders. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

Message frequency: Varies. Expect about 1–10 messages per week.

Cost: Msg & data rates may apply.

Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will get one confirmation message and nothing after that. Reply START to opt back in.

Help: Reply HELP for help, or contact the program owner directly.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

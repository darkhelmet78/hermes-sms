Privacy Policy – Hermes Home Alerts

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hermes Home Alerts, a private, non-commercial home automation SMS notification program operated by Robert Desselles (Folsom, Louisiana), collects, uses, and protects information from people who use our sign-up form or receive our text messages from (985) 615-2937.

Information we collect

We collect the name and email address of each person who submits our sign-up form. A mobile phone number is collected only from people who choose to opt in to text alerts, through the form or by texting START, along with the date, time, and method of consent. Providing a phone number and agreeing to text messages is optional.

How we use information

Phone numbers are used only to send the automated home alerts and reminders described in our SMS Terms & Conditions. Names and email addresses are used only to manage the household contact list. We do not use any information for marketing.

SMS consent and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services (such as our SMS provider) used only to deliver messages. Sign-up form submissions are delivered to the program operator by email through a form-processing service and are not used for any other purpose.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, about 1–10 messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention and security

Phone numbers and consent records are stored on a private, secured home server and kept only while the recipient remains opted in. They are deleted after opt-out.

Contact

Questions about this policy: rdesselles@gmail.com, or reply HELP to any message.

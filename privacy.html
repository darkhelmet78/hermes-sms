Privacy Policy – Hermes Home Alerts

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hermes Home Alerts, a private home automation SMS notification program operated by Robert Desselles (Folsom, Louisiana), collects, uses, and protects information from people who receive its text messages at (985) 615-2937.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone number of each household member who gives consent to receive messages, and the date that consent was given.

How we use information

Phone numbers are used only to send automated home alerts and reminders as described in our SMS Terms & Conditions. We do not use them for marketing.

SMS consent and sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Data retention and security

Phone numbers are stored on a private, secured home server and kept only while the recipient remains opted in. They are deleted after opt-out.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by replying HELP to any message.
